Search

Plain is ninja-keys' default: a regular expression over titles and keywords. Fields is catsearchSearcher(): the same bare words, plus fields, paths up the tree and completions under the box (Tab takes the first).

Try

  • bare words: titles and keywords, every level
  • every page anywhere below Rendering
  • published pages directly in Profiling
  • recent GPU material
  • alternatives, grouped, within one idea
  • a half-typed path: the completions show what can follow
  • matches titles and keywords
  • a regular expression
